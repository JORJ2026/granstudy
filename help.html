Help & Parents’ Guide

How do I install it like an app?

iPhone / iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android / Chrome / Edge: tap the Install app button on the home page, or use the browser menu.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first visit the games are saved on your device. Looking up a word definition needs internet.

There is no sound

Check the ⚙️ Settings and make sure Sound is on, then check your device’s volume and silent switch. Tap anywhere once — browsers wait for a tap before playing sound.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

On this device only. Use Settings → Download backup and then Restore backup on the other device.

How do I reset everything?

Settings → Erase everything. This cannot be undone, so download a backup first if you might want it.

Can I make the text bigger or calm down animations?

Yes — Settings has Text size, Dark / light theme and Reduce motion.

Set a daily goal or break reminders

In Settings choose a daily study goal in minutes. The ring on the home screen fills up as you study. You can also turn on gentle break reminders.

Something is broken

Open Settings → About & diagnostics and tap Copy diagnostics to share what happened. Reloading the page usually helps; if the app looks out of date, close all tabs and open it again to get the latest version.
