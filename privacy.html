Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Gran’s Study Time has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share anything about you or your children.

What is saved on your device

Scores, streaks, study minutes, settings and game progress are saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device only. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data, or using Settings → Erase everything, removes them.

Things that talk to the internet

WhatWhy
Google FontsLoads the Roboto font. If it is blocked or offline, a system font is used instead. Google may see your IP address when this loads.
Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev)Only when you ask for a word’s definition in Practice. Only the word being looked up is sent.
Your device’s speech voicesWords are read aloud using your browser’s built-in text-to-speech.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children: there is no chat, no sign-in, no purchases and no links to other sites inside the games.

Backups

You can download or restore a backup file of your progress from Settings. The file is created on your device and goes nowhere unless you send it yourself.
